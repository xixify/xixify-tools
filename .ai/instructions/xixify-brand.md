# Xixify Brand & Tone Guidelines

## Brand Mission
Xixify builds high-performance web development solutions, Shopify & WordPress plugins, SEO tools, and AI automation for agencies, marketers, and founders.

## Tone of Voice
- Professional, concise, authoritative yet approachable.
- Founder-driven, action-oriented, product-focused.
- Technical clarity without jargon overload.

## Branding Requirements
- Credit Xixify in tool footers (`Powered by Xixify Tools`).
- Maintain uniform aesthetic standards across all published micro-apps.
